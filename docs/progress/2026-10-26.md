The infrastructure work targets correct accounting, recovery and inspectable decisions.
A successful execution checkpoint is not a successful-alpha discovery checkpoint.

## Research question that emerged

A recorded campaign snapshot contained 179 completed candidates and one still in flight.
177 of the completed candidates fell below the current fitness floor. These counts include
related variants, and failed checks overlap. They are not independent discovery trials.

This motivates a specific question: **does the refinement process spend too much budget on
raising Sharpe without resolving fitness, trading intensity and other blocking checks?**

The next step is to inspect parent/child decisions and prepare a bounded comparison against
the current method. No new acceptance threshold has been introduced, no controlled comparison
has yet established an improvement, and no out-of-sample effectiveness is claimed.
